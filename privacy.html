Privacy & Cookies

CleanTranscribe runs entirely in your web browser. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your text, transcripts, or clipboard data on our servers. All processing happens locally on your device.

We do not use cookies or analytics. There are no third-party tracking scripts included in this application.

The "Copy" button uses your browser's Clipboard API to copy text locally. No content is sent to external services by this action.

If you choose to follow external links (for example to GitHub or donation services), those services are subject to their own privacy policies.
